ARTICLE II - THE LOAN

  Section 2.1  Principal.
    Lender has advanced to Borrower the original principal sum of Twenty-Four
    Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($24,500,000), bearing interest at a
    fixed rate of six and twenty-five hundredths percent (6.25%) per annum,
    payable in equal monthly installments aggregating One Million Eight Hundred
    Forty-Two Thousand Dollars ($1,842,000) per annum (the "Annual Debt
    Service").

  Section 2.2  Maturity Date.
    The entire unpaid principal balance, together with all accrued and unpaid
    interest and all other sums due under the Loan Documents, shall be due and
    payable in full on July 1, 2029 (the "Maturity Date"). Not later than one
    hundred eighty (180) days prior to the Maturity Date, Borrower shall notify
    Lender in writing of its intention to repay the Loan in full, to seek an
    extension, or to refinance. Failure to deliver such notice shall not extend
    the Maturity Date.


ARTICLE VI - APPRAISALS AND PROPERTY VALUE

  Section 6.4  Appraisals.
    Lender may, at Borrower's expense but not more frequently than once in any
    twelve (12) month period absent an Event of Default, obtain an updated
    appraisal of the Property prepared by an appraiser selected by Lender and
    conforming to the requirements of Title XI of FIRREA. Until such time as an
    updated appraisal is delivered, the Loan-to-Value Ratio shall be deemed not
    tested for the relevant period, and no such deemed non-testing shall be
    construed as compliance with Section 7.3(c) or as a waiver of any right of
    Lender thereunder.


ARTICLE VII - FINANCIAL REPORTING AND COVENANTS

  Section 7.1  Financial Statements.
    (a)  Borrower shall deliver to Lender, within forty-five (45) days after the
         close of each fiscal quarter, a reporting package comprising a current
         rent roll, an operating statement for the trailing twelve months, a
         certificate of property insurance then in force, and evidence of
         payment of real estate taxes.

    (b)  Each operating statement shall be prepared on a basis consistent with
         that of the preceding period and shall separately state effective gross
         income and total operating expenses.

    (c)  "Net Operating Income" means effective gross income for the applicable
         period less total operating expenses for such period, computed without
         add-back, exclusion or reclassification of any expense item that
         Borrower characterizes as capital, extraordinary or non-recurring,
         unless such treatment has been approved by Lender in writing prior to
         delivery of the statement. Where a statement delivered by Borrower
         reports a Net Operating Income differing from the amount so computed,
         the amount computed under this Section 7.1(c) shall govern for all
         purposes under this Agreement, and Borrower shall upon request deliver
         a written reconciliation of the difference.

  Section 7.2  Books and Records.
    Borrower shall maintain complete books and records at the Property and shall
    permit Lender or its agents to examine and audit the same upon not less than
    five (5) Business Days' notice.

  Section 7.3  Financial Covenants.
    (a)  The covenants set forth in this Section 7.3 shall be tested quarterly as
         of the last day of each fiscal quarter, on a trailing twelve month
         basis, using the figures derived from the reporting package delivered
         under Section 7.1.

    (b)  Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Borrower shall maintain, as of each test
         date, a ratio of Net Operating Income to Annual Debt Service of not less
         than 1.25 to 1.00. Net Operating Income for this purpose shall be
         determined in accordance with Section 7.1(c).

    (c)  Loan-to-Value Ratio. Borrower shall not permit the ratio of the
         outstanding principal balance of the Loan to the appraised value of the
         Property, as established by the most recent appraisal delivered under
         Section 6.4, to exceed seventy-five percent (75%).

    (d)  Occupancy. Borrower shall maintain physical occupancy of the Property of
         not less than eighty-five percent (85%), measured by leased rentable
         square feet as a proportion of total rentable square feet as shown on
         the rent roll delivered under Section 7.1(a).

  Section 7.4  Notice of Non-Compliance.
    Borrower shall notify Lender in writing within five (5) Business Days after
    becoming aware of any failure to comply with Section 7.3, whether or not
    Lender has delivered notice of such failure.


ARTICLE VIII - INSURANCE

  Section 8.1  Required Coverage.
    (a)  Borrower shall keep the Property insured against loss by fire and other
         hazards covered by a standard extended coverage endorsement in an amount
         not less than Twenty-Five Million Dollars ($25,000,000), written by a
         carrier rated not lower than A- (Excellent) by A.M. Best.

    (b)  Each policy shall name Lender as mortgagee and loss payee under a
         standard non-contributory mortgagee clause and shall provide that it may
         not be cancelled or materially modified without thirty (30) days' prior
         written notice to Lender.

    (c)  Borrower shall deliver a renewal certificate evidencing coverage
         conforming to this Section 8.1 not later than thirty (30) days prior to
         the expiration of the policy then in force. Any lapse in required
         coverage shall constitute an immediate Event of Default without cure
         period.

  Section 8.2  Proceeds.
    All insurance proceeds in excess of Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars
    ($250,000) shall be paid to Lender and applied to restoration or to the
    outstanding principal balance at Lender's election.
